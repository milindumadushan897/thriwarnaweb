Case study 01

Meta Ads performance case study

Ishu Fashion

Campaign period
1–28 August 2026
Industry
Online Fashion & Retail
Service
Meta Ads Management

The brief

Ishu Fashion is a Sri Lankan online fashion business specializing in women's frocks. The business had been using boosted posts to generate WhatsApp inquiries, but the approach was producing messages at a relatively high cost, with only around 10% of inquiries converting into sales.

The goal was to improve advertising efficiency, attract more purchase-ready inquiries, and turn a limited advertising budget into more orders and revenue.

Our approach

Thriwarna moved the campaign management from basic post boosting to a structured Meta Ads Manager strategy.

We rebuilt the campaign structure, refined audience targeting, leveraged Meta's Advantage+ capabilities, and managed multiple campaigns to identify and optimize better-performing opportunities. We also provided creative consultation to support campaign performance, while Ishu Fashion handled the video creation, editing, and publishing.

The focus was on reducing the cost of generating WhatsApp conversations and improving the journey from inquiry to purchase, without relying on discounts or promotional offers.

The outcome

During the campaign period, the optimized advertising approach delivered measurable results:

LKR 6,541Total advertising spend
479WhatsApp conversations generated
LKR 13.66Average cost per WhatsApp conversation
263Orders generated from Meta-driven inquiries
54.9%Inquiry-to-order conversion rate
LKR 522,000+Revenue attributed to Meta-generated inquiries
